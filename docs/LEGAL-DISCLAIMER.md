This repository provides general educational decision support for creator monetization and payout architecture.

It is not:

- legal advice
- tax advice
- accounting advice
- banking advice
- investment advice
- a guarantee of platform approval
- a guarantee that a payment method will be shown to a specific buyer
- a guarantee that a payout will be accepted by a bank

## Platform information

Platform:

- country support
- fees
- payout methods
- KYC
- reserves
- settlement schedules
- product eligibility
- refund and dispute policies

can change without notice. The repository review date is not a substitute for checking the live official agreement during registration.

`official` means an official source supports the core claim. It does not mean every creator, product or transaction will be approved.

## Taiwan tax

Tax treatment depends on facts that this application does not collect, including:

- residency
- business establishment
- product or service classification
- platform and contract structure
- buyer and audience location
- advertiser location
- profit contribution
- invoice obligations
- other household income
- prior registrations and filings

The NT$50,000 monthly service-sales warning is a registration review gate, not a complete tax determination.

Foreign payout origin does not automatically make all income “overseas income.”

## Cross-border and mainland China

The project rejects:

- private collection agents
- underground exchange
- borrowed accounts
- false addresses
- identity mismatches
- source-of-funds concealment
- unverified crypto off-ramps

Cross-border availability shown in a platform help page may still depend on buyer device, region, product, currency, risk review and merchant approval.

## Foreign entities

A foreign company can create:

- annual filings
- accounting cost
- banking obligations
- beneficial-owner disclosure
- local tax exposure
- Taiwan reporting and substance questions

Do not create an entity solely to imitate eligibility without real governance and professional review.

## Sensitive data

Do not enter or submit credentials, identity documents, bank details, tax IDs or verification codes into this project. Registration occurs only on official third-party domains.

## Professional review trigger

Seek a Taiwan CPA, bookkeeper, tax authority or qualified lawyer before launch when:

- monthly service sales approach NT$50,000
- monthly goods sales approach NT$100,000
- B2B invoices are required
- a platform freezes or reserves funds
- chargebacks rise
- a foreign entity is considered
- mainland China payment or data rules are involved
- overseas/basic-income thresholds may apply
- income classification is uncertain
